Who provides the service

Delta Bridge manages your payment from quote to confirmation. Regulated payment and exchange partners hold, convert and pay out the money, and the partner on your payment is named in your quote.

How funds are checked

Every business is verified before its first payment: the company, its owners and where its funds come from. Each deposit is screened before it is converted.

Where money is held

With the regulated partner handling your payment, never with Delta Bridge itself. Your quote tells you who that is.

Questions

The questions we get first.

Ask us something else
Who can use Delta Bridge?

Registered businesses. The company, its owners and where its funds come from are verified before the first payment. Tell us where you are registered and we will confirm on the first call.

Which stablecoins and networks do you accept?

USDT and USDC. We confirm the network with your quote, so nothing is sent to the wrong place.

Can you go the other way, from local currency to stablecoins?

Yes. You send local currency by bank transfer and receive USDT or USDC at your wallet. The currencies and amounts are confirmed in your quote.

How long does a payment take?

It depends on the currency and the receiving bank. You get an arrival time in writing with your quote, before you send anything.

What does it cost?

One fee per payment, shown in your quote alongside the rate. Nothing is added afterwards.

Send us a payment to quote.

Tell us the amount and where it is going. You get a rate, a fee and a time back.
