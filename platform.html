01  Receive

A deposit address for your business.

Send USDT or USDC from any wallet or exchange. It is checked, then credited to your balance.

  • One address per asset and network
  • A warning before you send on the wrong network
  • Checked before it credits
02  Pay out

See the rate before you say yes.

Type the amount. You see what they receive, the fee and when it arrives. The rate is held while you review.

  • Rate held on a visible countdown
  • Fee shown before you confirm
  • Paid on local rails with your reference
03  Approve

Two people, one payment.

Set who can create payouts, who can approve them and how much each person can release.

  • Limits per person, per payout and per day
  • Approve from your phone with Face ID
  • Every approval recorded against a name
