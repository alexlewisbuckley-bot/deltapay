Why we exist

Getting paid was the easy part.

Why we started
Delta Pay

We kept meeting businesses that were being paid in stablecoins and getting stuck at the bank. So we built the service we would want to use ourselves.

A customer in another country can send a stablecoin payment in a minute. Turning that into money your supplier, landlord or payroll provider will accept is where it goes wrong.

Frozen transfers, vague fees, and a bank asking where the money came from. Delta Pay does that one job properly. The funds are checked, converted at a rate you have already agreed, and paid out on local bank rails, so the person receiving them sees an ordinary domestic transfer.

We work with businesses that move real money and need to explain every payment to an accountant, an auditor or a bank.
